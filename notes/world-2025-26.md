Goal (Davide): a worldwide historic career of the 2025-26 season, squads from SWOS 2020 (Playaveli agrees), real
modern formats (UEFA league phases, Libertadores/Sudamericana, then AFC/CAF/CONCACAF/OFC, national teams last).
Decisions: calendar-year leagues = **2025**; European places from season 2 = **simulated UEFA ranking** (country and
club coefficients); national teams after a clubs release; inside CLASSIC CAREERS a **first sub-menu by continent**.

Private material (never in the repo): `internal/world2526/` — `mkworld2526.py` (SWOS 2020 files -> world 5 career
packs, method `records`), `run.sh` (convert -> check -> build; `SCOPE=all|europe|mix|firstN`, `ITALY_C=1`), `t8/`,

## Engine limits found
- **Global team numbers < 2000**; the game's national teams sit at 1587-1729 and the mod's save blocks at 1730-1846:
  a world holds about **1587 clubs** (league + cup-only) unless the global space is moved. 2025-26 needs ~1400-1600.
- **Contest ids**: one byte, 0x80-0xFE minus the reserved ones = ~79 free; ~70 national cups + the new
  international cups need more -> make cup ids per world (worlds never run together) or drop cups of tiny countries.
- **Start year**: mkseason accepted < 2000 only; 2025 works in game (`CAMP. 2025/26`, then `2026 FINE CAMP.`).
- **Countries in one menu**: a world with 70 countries exits to DOS when its country list opens; 43-44 work (the
  game's Europe lists 43) -> the continent sub-menu (Davide's request) is also the fix. DONE (commit 25037cc): a world
  over several continents lists them (game names EUROPA / SUDAMERICA / NORDAMERICA / ASIA / AFRICA / OCEANIA, menu ids
  251 down) under its CLASSIC CAREERS entry and in VIEW WORLD; w_lvl accepts depth 4. Europe 2025-26 has 42 countries +
  the small UEFA nations of slot 031 (one pseudo-country in the prototype; as separate countries Europe would pass 44
  -> two lists, or those clubs as cup-only entrants).
- **Team file > 64000 B** (Italy A 20 + B 20 + C 3x20 = 100 clubs): LoadFile reads the whole file into
  teamFileBuffer (64014 B) before checking 64000 -> careerworld.big_team_buffer moves it to the obj2 tail
  (106496 B, 155 clubs, 31 fixups, both checks raised). A Serie C club career (100-club Italy) starts and plays
  season 1 to the end.
- **36 clubs in one group** (UEFA league phase): a one-group tourney of 36 starts, but the fixtures pair a club with
  itself (ARSENAL - ARSENAL) and the table screen is corrupted; 24 is the most the group/league table screen shows (24
  rows fill the screen). -> the league phase needs our own standings (36), our own 8-matchday calendar from the draw
  and a 2-page table (1-24 = the qualification zone, 25-36).
- **Parallel groups** (Serie C 3x20, Primera Federacion 2x20): the engine has one chain of divisions per country;
  the prototype puts the 3 groups as divisions 3-5 of the chain (promotion between them is wrong). Real rules =
  a season-end hook that reassigns divisions (3 winners + play-off winner up, relegated from B spread over groups).

### 3. League phase (UEFA format 2024-25 onward) — study + prototype (2026-10-15)
Format (UEFA regulations 2025-26 art. 18-19): 36 clubs, 4 pots of 9; 8 matches each (one home + one away opponent from
every pot), no club of its own country, at most 2 opponents of one country; 8 matchdays, one table of 36. Ranking:
points, then goal difference, goals, away goals, wins, away wins, opponents' points, opponents' goal difference,
opponents' goals, disciplinary points, club coefficient (during the phase only the first five, then alphabetical).
1-8 to the round of 16, 9-24 two-leg play-offs (9/10 v 23/24, 11/12 v 21/22, 13/14 v 19/20, 15/16 v 17/18, seeded club
home in the 2nd leg), 25-36 out. R16: 1/2 v the winners of 15/16 v 17/18, 3/4 v 13/14 v 19/20, 5/6 v 11/12 v 21/22,
7/8 v 9/10 v 23/24 (seeded club home in the 2nd leg), then a fixed bracket (QF, SF), single final. Europa League:
same. Conference League: 6 matches, 6 pots of 6, one opponent per pot (3 home, 3 away).

**Engine facts (type-2 group stage = a DIY league in DIY_competitionStart):**
- A career type-2 contest lives in a slot buffer of **0xB52 = diyFileBufferCopy (0x41F) + DIY_competitionStart
  (0x733)** (cseg_8B7EA copies by type: league 733h, type-1 cup 443h, type-2 B52h), so it is saved whole in the .CAR.
  diyFileBufferCopy: contest list +59h (64 bytes), team words +99h (64), group of each index +119h (64), stages from
  +15Dh, 0x76 each, at most 6 (0x15D + 6 x 0x76 = 0x3F9). [53h] = current stage.
- DIY_competitionStart: **64 team rows** at +2B3h + 18 r (+2B3h team dword, +2B7h played, +2B9h won, +2BBh drawn,
  +2BDh lost, +2BFh GF, +2C1h GA, +2C3h points; cseg_26395 clears 64 rows) -> 36 rows fit; group slot lists at +0EDh
  (64 bytes, group g at g*n: row indices); sorted standings at +6Dh (64 words = row offsets, group g at g*2n).
- Counters (cseg_2653B at the stage start, cseg_8922B after every match): [4Fh] groups, [51h] clubs per group, [5Bh]
  current group, [1CBh] pair index, [1C5h] = n/2 pairs per day, [1C7h] matches per day, [1C9h] matches today, [1BFh]
  day done, [1C1h] day, [1C3h] days, [69h]/[6Bh] pair indices per cycle / left, [5Fh] cycles left (0 = stage over).
  Match order: pair k of group 0, pair k of group 1, ..., then pair k+1.
- **Pairing = cseg_89758**: A3 = per-size table dseg_11F356[n-2] (2..24), entry [1CBh] = 2 slots; slot -> row via the
  slot list; [5Fh] odd swaps home/away. Every user of a fixture goes through it: the match itself (cseg_88A12), the
  career calendar builder (cseg_8B8D9, simulating the counters), the fixture lists of the screens.
- Results: cseg_88A12 (career, preset, season-end simulation) adds the result to the rows (A3 home, A4 away, D1/D2
  goals) then calls the sort cseg_883DD (per group: points, GD, GF, name).
- Career calendar of a type-2 stage: [173h] days of the stage (cseg_2573C: (n-1) x meetings), spread over the months
  by cseg_260A9 into the stage's date table +175h; cseg_8C2AE makes one calendar entry per day.
- Group end: cseg_8A2CE writes the qualifiers (q per group + best next) into +59h; then hist_draw (cseg_26DFC, the
  shuffle call) orders each knockout round.
- Group table screen (DIYLeagueTournament16BeforeDraw): header = 'GRUPPO A..H' from the table cseg_49E7A when [4Fh]
  > 1; 18 rows on one page with scroll arrows; rows 1..q blue (q = qualifiers per group = 8), the others red.

**Design (chosen): 2 groups of 18 as the engine's container, everything else ours (tools/lp36.py).**
The re-partition idea works as planned, and better: the pairing does not depend on the groups at all, so the groups
are only two pages of one table.
1. lp_days: stage 1 has 8 days (career calendar).
2. lp_start (after cseg_2653B): [69h] = [6Bh] = 72 (x 2 groups = 144 matches), [1C3h] = 8, clear the away records.
   Note: cseg_258E4 copies the contest id into DIY+2Dh only at its end -> test diyFileBufferCopy+2Dh there.
3. lp_pair (entry of cseg_89758): match m = 2 [1CBh] + [5Bh] of the draw LP_CAL (day = m / 18) -> rows directly.
4. lp_res (in cseg_88A12): away goals / away wins per club in unused rows 44-47 (saved with the slot).
5. lp_sort (entry of cseg_883DD): one ranking of 36 with the UEFA keys (opponents' figures from the draw) written into
   +6Dh over both groups: page A = places 1-18, page B = 19-36.
6. lp_gend (after cseg_8A2CE): list[0..15] = play-off ties in bracket order, places 1-8 parked at list[40..47].
7. lp_draw (from hist_draw): keep every order; round of 16 = (play-off winner k, seeded k), seeded order 1 8 3 5 | 2 7
   4 6 (QF 1v8, 3v5, 2v7, 4v6 as the 2024-25 bracket).
The draw itself: lp36.draw (Python, build time) = random bijections per pot pair with the country rules, then 8 perfect
matchings (backtracking); lp36.check asserts every rule.

**Harness: the "random exit to DOS" of phase 0 is explained.** swostest started DOSBox-X with `-time-limit 14400`:
the limit counts EMULATED seconds, and in turbo 4 emulated hours pass in ~4.5 real minutes (dynamic core; longer on
the normal core) -> DOSBox-X closes cleanly (exit code 0, also with SWOSTEST_NOEXIT), at the same point for the same
run, whatever the build (a control build without lp36 stopped at the same pause). Now 100000000.
