# Serie C play-off, exact C.U. 209/A (4.2, S3 step 2)

Source: FIGC Comunicato Ufficiale N. 209/A (Consiglio Federale of 27 April 2026, published 6 May 2026), Allegato A, art. 49 NOIF,
c) Lega Pro, II-VI (the play-off part is the same in the old and the new column). Full text, URL and the rule table:
`internal/handoff/serie-c-cu209a.md` (private); the cup-place code is `tools/cuprule.c`, ONE implementation used by the season-end
simulation (gplay.c) and by the career club's contest (dplay.c).

## The format (World 2026-27, Italy)
28 clubs: 2nd-10th of the three Serie C groups + the Coppa Italia Serie C winner (or whoever takes the cup's place). Group phase:
R1 5v10, 6v9, 7v8 of each group (one match at the better placed, a draw -> the better placed), R2 the three winners + the 4th, best v
worst and the other two at the better placed's ground. National phase: R1 (10 clubs) seeds = the three 3rd-placed, the cup place and the
best group winner, the other five drawn, two legs, the seeds host the return (points, then goal difference, then the seed); R2 (8) seeds =
the three 2nd-placed and the best R1 winner, same rules. Final Four: drawn semi-finals and a final, two legs each, goal difference, then
extra time and penalties. The winner goes up to Serie B.

## The cup place (II.1 / II.2)
- The cup winner takes his own place unless he is precluded: 1st-3rd of his group, relegated directly (the last, or the LOWER club of a
  play-out pair whose play-out is not held because of the points gap), or in a play-out that is held.
- Precluded: the OTHER FINALIST takes the cup place unless the same applies to him; if it does, the place goes to the 4th of the
  cup WINNER's group.
- The club that takes the place from 4th-10th of its group leaves its group's play-off; the clubs below move up one place and the 11th of
  that group comes in (so a 4th leaves the R2 seat to the old 5th, and R1 becomes 6v11, 7v10, 8v9). From the 11th down nothing scrolls.
- Not modelled: a club renouncing the play-off (II.1 f).
- An old save without the finalist (it was not kept before 4.2) counts the finalist as precluded: the 4th of the winner's group.

## Where it runs
- AI seasons: gplay.c plays `c_po` (the engine spec ita_s3) at the season end per spec round.
- The career club in the C play-off: the contest is played in slot 4 by dplay.c (28 clubs, merged group rounds, real calendar) with the same
  rule (the one function) and the scroll applied to the places it lists. A save made inside a C phase by an older build finishes on dplay
  with its saved clubs.
- Tests: `gplay_cuprule` (24 host cases), `world_s3_c_exact_finalist / _own / _fourth` (the contest state after the scroll), `world_s3_c_ai`,
  `world_s3_oldsave_c`.

## Same rule on both paths (structure)
The season-end simulation (gplay.c, `cup_code`) and the career contest (dplay.c, `cup_place` / `c_lend` / `serie_c`) both call `cuprule.c`'s
`cup_rule` on a view of the same final tables, so for the same table they get the same cup place and the same scroll. The scroll is
applied to the listed places identically: gplay's `place_club` takes a listed place p (1-based) at or below the scroll's first place to
the real place p + 1; dplay's `pm(g, p)` takes a listed place p (0-based, so p + 1 1-based) at or below it to the real place p + 1.
