## English

**SWOS 96/97 Mod 4.0: the World 2026-27 career, today's football as it really is**

*Football competitions, global and local, that no other game has, has ever had, or probably ever will.*

### What's new
- **World 2026-27**, a new historic career world: 91 countries, 2,385 clubs with their real squads at the end of the
  summer 2026 transfer window, 264 competitions. In CLASSIC CAREERS choose WORLD 2026-27, or use the new green
  **CAREER 2026-27** button on the main menu (next to CAREER): it opens the career menu, and its CHOOSE TEAM goes
  straight to the world's continents.
  - Every league in its real 2026-27 format: groups, splits (title and relegation groups), Apertura / Clausura,
    conferences, promotion, title and placement play-offs, play-outs, real relegations; Costa Rica, Honduras and Panama with
    two tournaments a season and their real final phases; Italy down to the real Serie D (9 groups) with its play-offs and the **Coppa Italia Serie D** on its real bracket.
  - Every national cup and league cup from its real round, the 13 **Brazilian state championships**,
    their tables in colour.
  - The UEFA cups with the league phase, and **12 new continental competitions**: Copa Libertadores, Copa
    Sudamericana, Recopa, CONCACAF Champions Cup, AFC Champions League Elite, Champions League Two and Challenge
    League, CAF Champions League, Confederation Cup and Super Cup, OFC Champions League, and the FIFA
    **Intercontinental Cup** (the real stepladder). Clubs of associations that are not in the world play as guests
    with their real squads.
  - Every table in colour (titles, cups, promotion, play-offs, play-outs, relegation) with one legend at the top left;
    every competition has a FORMAT page in plain words, in 4 languages.
- **WINNERS page**: in a competition's STAT menu, the season's titles beyond the table (play-offs, Apertura /
  Clausura, state champions) with their winners, TO BE PLAYED or IN PROGRESS; after the season end, last season's.
- **Quick season end**: the other countries' competitions are simulated without being shown one by one (your own country's competitions and the European cups still show, as in the original game), with a progress line (SIMULATING OTHER COUNTRIES... n/N).
- **One engine for all of it**: groups, splits, play-offs and cups share the same rules (one knockout rule for the
  mod's play-offs: extra time, then the better-placed club goes through; no replays).
- **Europe 1984-85** (DaniSwos's database): tables in colour and FORMAT pages too.
- **One-click install** (Chrome or Edge): choose the game folder and the patcher does the rest, keeping a backup of
  the files it changes; it can also copy the game from the CD. The page is in 4 languages.

### Install or update
- The patcher page (attached below) upgrades the original game and every published version (CD and GOG, also the
  English SWOS++ version). Step-by-step guide: [English](https://worldofswos.com/en/install/) ·
  [Italiano](https://worldofswos.com/it/install/).
- If the game is in Program Files (GOG Galaxy's default folder), the browser won't open it: copy the game folder
  elsewhere, patch the copy, copy its contents back over the original (Windows asks for administrator permission:
  accept), or use the manual mode.
- Start a new career to play the World 2026-27. Careers saved with the original game and with every earlier version of the mod (1.x, 2.x, 3.x: 1996-97 and Europe
  1984-85) load and play on.

### Known limits (declared)
- **European places**: in some seasons one of the 108 European places goes to a club of a different association from
  the one the access list names; the number of clubs in each cup and the holders' places are always right (fixed in
  the upcoming 4.0.1).
- **Copa Libertadores**: the group thirds' move to the Sudamericana play-off is simulated (played in the upcoming 4.0.1).
- **AFC**: the Champions League Elite league stage and the Challenge League groups are simulated; your club joins the
  Elite at the round of 16 and the Challenge League at the quarter-finals.
- After a job offer letter a few stray pixels can appear on the menu buttons until the next match: cosmetic only.
- National cups of the World 2026-27 keep their 2026-27 entrants every season: a club promoted or relegated into another
  division doesn't join that division's cup (e.g. Mestre promoted to Serie C doesn't play the Coppa Italia Serie C);
  the upcoming 4.0.1 draws each season's entrants from the current divisions.
- In Serie D groups with an odd number of clubs, a club that rests on the last match day does not play its Poule
  Scudetto or group play-off: it is decided by simulation (fixed in the upcoming 4.0.1).
- The World 2026-27 has no national teams yet (planned for the upcoming 4.2).

### Limiti noti (dichiarati)
- **Posti europei**: in alcune stagioni uno dei 108 posti europei va a un club di una federazione diversa da quella

- **AFC**: la fase a campionato della Champions League Elite e i gironi della Challenge League sono simulati; il tuo
  club entra nella Elite dagli ottavi e nella Challenge League dai quarti.
- Dopo una lettera di offerta di lavoro possono comparire alcuni pixel sporchi sui pulsanti dei menu fino alla
  partita successiva: solo estetico.
- Le coppe nazionali del Mondo 2026-27 tengono ogni stagione le partecipanti del 2026-27: una squadra promossa o
  retrocessa non entra nella coppa della nuova categoria (es. il Mestre promosso in Serie C non gioca la Coppa Italia

- Nei gironi di Serie D con un numero dispari di squadre, una squadra che riposa all'ultima giornata non gioca la
  Poule Scudetto o il play-off del girone: vengono simulati (corretto nella prossima 4.0.1).
- Il Mondo 2026-27 non ha ancora le nazionali (previste nella prossima 4.2).
