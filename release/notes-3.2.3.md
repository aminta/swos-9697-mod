## English

**SWOS 96/97 Mod 3.2.3: KITS button aligned in the English version**

- Pre-match screen, English executable (also the SWOS++ version): the **KITS AUTO / KITS SET** button now starts
  exactly at the left edge of the player names and no longer touches the check/X mark; the text is centred in it. In
  the English game the player list sits 6 pixels further right than in the Italian, French and German ones, and the
  button was placed for those. Reported by Marco.
- Only the English executable changes (CD, GOG and SWOS++). Italian, French and German are the same as 3.2.2.
- Updating: the patcher upgrades 3.2.2 and every earlier version (CD and GOG). Saves are not touched.
