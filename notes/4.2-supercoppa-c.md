# 4.2 Supercoppa Serie C: a mini-league stage on the fixture port

Design: `internal/handoff/supercoppa-c-design-20261116.md`; rules and sources: `internal/handoff/supercoppa-c-spec.md`;
interface: `tools/fpml.h` (frozen). CONVENTIONS 27.

## What the player gets
The winners of Serie C groups A, B and C (World 2026-27) play a three-club mini-league after the last league day:
units L + 1, L + 2, L + 3 (L = 128: the first half of May). Matchday 1 is drawn (who rests, who plays away); the
matchday-1 winner rests on matchday 2 (after a draw, the away side); matchday 3 pairs the two clubs that have not
met. 3 / 1 / 0 points, a level score stands, ties: goal difference, goals for, away goals for, lots. A career club
that won its group plays its two matches in slot 4; any other career gets the three matches simulated at the
season's end. The winner goes to the WINNERS page (lane 2, `tools/fpmlv.c`).

## Generic by construction
The engine-facing code knows no division, file, club or name. The Supercoppa is ONE row of the Italian pack's league
competition:

```json
"mini_leagues": [{"key": "supercoppa_c", "names": {"*": ["SUPERCOPPA SERIE C", "SUPERCOPPA C"]},
  "entrants": [[2, 1], [3, 1], [4, 1]], "schedule": "winner_rests", "legs": 1, "points": [3, 1, 0],
  "tiebreak": ["goal_difference", "goals_for", "away_goals_for", "draw_lots"], "units": [1, 2, 3],
  "record": {"*": ["SUPERCOPPA SERIE C"]}}]
```

- `entrants`: `[division, place]` of the final tables (the table's own place, as gplay's place_club kind 4), 2..4.
- `schedule`: `winner_rests` (3 clubs, one leg) or `fixed` (the engine's round-robin calendar, up to 6 matches).
- `points`: win, draw, loss - the single constant of the 3 / 1 / 0 decision. `tiebreak`: after the points.
- `units`: one offset from the league's last unit per matchday.
`tools/fpad.py ml_table()` compiles the rows into FP_MLT (struct fp_mlrow, 34 B) and refuses what the port cannot run.
A build has the port when its data has such a row (release builds too) or with FPORT=1 (test builds).

## Flow
1. The career league's last day (gp_lend). `gp_need_pre` asks GP_PNEED: if the career club holds an entrant place,
   the other entrants' divisions are simulated now and kept (gplay's early simulations, restored at the season end -
   the clubs that play the stage are the ones promoted). Then `gp_pre_c` calls GP_PLEND = `fp_ml_open(0)`: the
   instance opens, the seed (a hash of the entrants, their points and the season; FPML_SEED in tests) draws matchday 1.
   (The hook is in gp_pre_c, not gp_lend_c: a group winner has no play-off, and gp_lend_c runs only for a club with one.)
2. `progress()`: the matches the policy has decided, in order. One without the career club is scored at once (the
   port's own hash of its match id, no decider). The career club's becomes one port fixture and the stage waits; the
   port installs it in slot 4 at the advance before its unit. The next fixture is queued only when the result is in
   (rule 5 of the port: one slot-4 tie at a time). A fixture the port refuses is simulated: the stage never stalls.
3. The result comes back through the port (fp_done -> sr_port_result_in -> h_done -> `fp_ml_result`): the winner of a
   level-NONE fixture is the score's (2 = level).
4. All matches played: the table, the winner, state 2, FP_MLDONE.
5. The season end (gp_end_c, after gp_season, before the S7 swap): GP_PEND = `fp_ml_open(1)`. No instance this season
   (career club not an entrant; a save from before 4.2 or loaded after L) -> opened, simulated, closed in one call.
   An open one is finished the same way. FP_MLDONE again for every decided instance (the WINNERS list is now this
   season's).

## Rule 6 (G3): the live condition
fport gives divisions with season-end play-offs an unconditional `[L, END]` slot-4 window. General rule: a club in a
running mini-league stage is not in its division's season-end play-offs, so for it the windows open to the season's
end are off (`fpad.c h_windows` + `fpml.c fp_ml_busy`). No Serie C case anywhere.

## Save
Old saves have no FP item: it reads zero, no instance, step 5 decides the season's edition. A corrupt ML block (tag,
version, count) is ignored; a corrupt instance (fp_ml_loaded: the engine does not reproduce the held schedule, a club
twice, a wrong row) is dropped alone; the port's own section is independent. The FP item grew 2408 -> 2528 B: the big
cache's save budget TC_CAP is computed from it by trailer.py.

## Hooks: gplay's one registry (MC 17/11)
No module writes a gplay hook dword: each registers in `tools/gphooks.py` (42-frnd's registry; lane 1's copy is gone)
and `patch.py` links every chain once, in the declared order (frnd 10, nazionali 20, supercoppa 30, fpad 90).
The Supercoppa C's four are registered as `nazionali`: GP_PNEED -> `fp_ml_hneed` (next FP_NNEED), GP_PLEND ->
`fp_ml_lend` (next FP_NLEND), GP_PEND -> `fp_ml_end` (next FP_NEND) - each does its work, then ALWAYS calls its next -
and GP_PRLINE -> lane 2's `fpv_rline_c`, with no next dword: the end of its chain (a later module there is refused
at link). `test_gphooks` links every combination and calls through the dwords.
GP_PLEND stays in gp_pre_c (a group winner has no play-off, gp_lend_c never runs for it); entrants are the tables'
own places, as gplay's place_club kind 4.

## Tests: what the gates need
- `FPML_DATA` = a world dir whose Italian pack has the row. A world_supc_* scenario runs with W2627REL_DATA = that
  same dir (fpmlgate sets and restores it): the harness's contest-name check reads its names from world_data(), and
  with another dir there 'SUPERCOPPA C' is "not a slot-4 contest name" (world_supc_rest / _loss, 17/11: harness).
- A stage decided at the season end logs MLOPEN / MLDONE after the season's last read and the next season's init
  wipes FP_ST's log (fpad.c arm(FL_SEASON)): the gate wants MLDONE once, or none with the wipe itself read (the
  next season's SEASON entry) - then the kept instance is the proof (state 2, 3 played, flags bit 0). This is what
  made world_supc_oldsave look flaky on 17/11: the game did the same in the FAIL and the PASS run; the batch's
  process still held the older 'exactly once' check.
- world_supc_off: without a row and without FPORT the exe and the DATA are the reference commit's, byte for byte.
  With FPORT=1 and no row the exe differs by design (the port's bands, FP item v2): the check there is FP_MLN = 0.

## TEMP markers: a release build refuses them (MC 17/11)
Every temporary patch below carries a `TEMP-<NAME>` literal and is declared at build time
(`tempmark.temp_marker('G1')` in fpad.patch, when the port links). `patch.py` checks after the last link step: a
release or RC build (no test switch, no HOSTTEST_SYMS, MODVER_TAG empty or RC*) with a marker is REFUSED, no exe is
written; any other build logs `exe: TEMP markers: ...`. `tools/tempmark.py KNOWN` is the list; `test_tempmark` keeps
it equal to the sources. Today: TEMP-BANDS (swreg) alone.
